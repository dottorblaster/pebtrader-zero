PebTrader Zero shows the state of your CardTrader Zero orders on your Pebble.

• Your orders at a glance: state, item count, total and counterparty
• Open an order for its timeline, totals, shipping method and tracking code
• Hold Down for your CardTrader Zero box: what's ready, what's on the way and when it arrives
• The last snapshot is cached on the watch, so it still works without your phone
• Your API token stays on your phone and is never sent to the watch

Requires a CardTrader account and an API token, which you paste into the app's
settings (CardTrader → Settings → API).

Built for Pebble Time 2 (Emery) and Pebble Round 2 (Gabbro). Open source:
https://github.com/dottorblaster/pebtrader-zero
